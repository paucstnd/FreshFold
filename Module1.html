About FreshFold

Revolutionizing laundry services since 2020

Our Story

FreshFold was founded in 2020 with a simple mission: to give people their time back. Our founder, Sarah Mitchell, realized how much time was being lost to laundry.

What started as a small pickup service with one van has grown into a thriving business serving thousands of customers across the city.

Today, we're proud to be the leading laundry pickup and delivery service in the area, but we've never lost sight of our core values: quality, convenience, and care.
